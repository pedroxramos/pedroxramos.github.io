about

I simulate water and wind around ships, offshore structures, bridges and rivers. I have worked in hydraulics and CFD since 2012, from bridge pier scour in Porto to wave and wind loading on offshore vessels, run on HPC clusters with thousands of cores.

I also build VR flow worlds that let clients walk through the results, and surrogate models that turn days of simulation into seconds.
